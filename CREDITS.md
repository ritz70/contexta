## 💖 Open-Source Technologies & Acknowledgments

We are grateful to the creators and maintainers of the open-source libraries that made Contexta possible:

- **[Next.js](https://nextjs.org/)** by Vercel — React framework for modern server-rendered applications.
- **[TipTap](https://tiptap.dev/) & [ProseMirror](https://prosemirror.net/)** by Marijn Haverbeke — Extensible, headless rich-text editing engine.
- **[Supabase](https://supabase.com/)** — PostgreSQL infrastructure, Auth, and Row-Level Security.
- **[Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)** — Utility-first styling architecture.
- **[Lucide Icons](https://lucide.dev/)** — Clean, harmonious icon set.
- **[Recharts](https://recharts.org/)** — Composable charting library for React.
- **[Vitest](https://vitest.dev/)** by the Vite team — Blazing-fast unit testing framework.
- **[Zod](https://zod.dev/)** by Colin McDonnell — TypeScript-first schema declaration and validation.
- **[Sonner](https://sonner.emilkowal.ski/)** by Emil Kowalski — Elegant toast notifications.
